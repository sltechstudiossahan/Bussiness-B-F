BUSINESS MANAGER - setup

1. Firebase Console > Authentication > Sign-in method > enable Email/Password.
2. Authentication > Users > Add user. Create your admin account yourself.
3. Copy that user's UID.
4. Firestore Database > Rules: replace ADMIN_UID in firestore.rules with that UID, then Publish.
5. Upload index.html to your hosting (Firebase Hosting is recommended).
6. Open the site on your phone and sign in with the admin email/password.

Data is stored separately:
users/{adminUid}/businesses/food/...
users/{adminUid}/businesses/farm/...

Important:
- Do not share your admin password.
- Do not publish permissive Firestore rules such as allow read, write: if true.
- This is a starter version with mobile dashboard, shops, items, income/sales,
  expenses and farm egg-production records. Additional workflows such as invoices,
  stock adjustments, suppliers, vaccination/mortality and PDF/Excel reports can be
  added in the next iteration.
